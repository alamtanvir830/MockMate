## Content Approach

### Reading & Writing

Stimuli are original passages of 40–130 words written specifically for this form. Topics draw from:
- Physical and life sciences (ecology, physics, chemistry, biology)
- Social sciences (economics, sociology, anthropology, political science)
- History and humanities (historiography, art history, literary criticism)
- Technology and engineering

No stimulus was sourced from real published articles, academic papers, or books. Topics are informed by publicly known scientific and historical facts, not reproduced texts.

### Math

All mathematical problems are independently constructed. Numeric values, variable names, and word-problem scenarios are original. The underlying mathematical concepts (linear equations, quadratic functions, Pythagorean theorem, etc.) are mathematical facts in the public domain and cannot be owned.

## Quality Assurance

Each question was reviewed against the following criteria before publication:

1. **No verbatim overlap** with any known commercial SAT prep question bank
2. **Mathematical correctness** — all answer keys and explanations verified
3. **Plausible distractors** — wrong answers are not trivially wrong; they represent common errors
4. **Skill tagging accuracy** — domain and skill classifications match the SAT Blueprint skill definitions
5. **Difficulty calibration** — Form 3 targets 10/10 MockMate difficulty; easy questions match SAT medium, hard questions challenge 1550+ scorers
