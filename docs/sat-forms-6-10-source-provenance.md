government actions), the underlying facts are in the public domain — only the specific

## Provenance Protocol for Future Source-Derived Passages

For any future passage derived from a primary source:

1. **Verify rights**: Confirm the source is a U.S. federal government work in the public domain,
   a statute, official court opinion, or historical text with confirmed public-domain status.
2. **Record source**: Document the exact title, institution, publication date, and URL.
3. **Record rights basis**: State why the source is authorized (e.g., "U.S. federal government work
   per 17 U.S.C. § 105 — not subject to copyright").
4. **Write original passage**: Create an original 25–150-word passage that communicates the ideas
   without reproducing distinctive protected language.
5. **Record transformation**: Note what was changed from the source.
6. **Reviewer verification**: A second reviewer confirms no protected language survived.
