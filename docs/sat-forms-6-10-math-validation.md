# SAT Forms 6–10 Math Validation

## Validation Protocol

Every Math question must pass these checks before being marked validated:

1. **Independent solve**: Solve without looking at the answer key.
2. **Substitution check**: Substitute the answer back into the original equation.
3. **Second method**: Solve using an alternate approach (algebraic vs. numeric, factoring vs. quadratic formula, etc.).
4. **Distractor verification**: Confirm each wrong answer is actually wrong by testing it.
5. **Domain check**: Verify no domain violations (no sqrt of negative for real answers, no division by zero).
6. **Extraneous root check**: For equations with squaring steps, verify no extraneous solutions are included.
7. **Unit check**: Confirm units are consistent and specified when relevant.
8. **Grid-in completeness**: All acceptable answer forms listed (fractions and decimals where applicable).
9. **Rounding specification**: When rounding is required, it is stated in the question.

## Known Math Risk Categories

These question types receive extra scrutiny:

- **Nonlinear systems**: Verify real solutions exist; check discriminant.
- **Quadratics**: Verify Vieta's formulas (sum/product of roots) are consistent with the equation.
- **Parameter questions**: Verify the constant/parameter interpretation is unambiguous.
- **Exponent equations**: Verify base restrictions and that extraneous solutions are excluded.
- **Geometry**: Verify all given measurements are consistent (no impossible triangles, etc.).
- **Probability**: Verify sample space is correctly identified; conditional probability tables sum correctly.
- **Grid-in fractions**: Confirm decimal equivalents listed; confirm no rounding needed for exact answers.
